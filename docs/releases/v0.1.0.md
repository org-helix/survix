The first release of **Survix**, the offline survey editor for Android from [Helix](https://github.com/org-helix).

Helix builds public-good software for the communities that mainstream tech leaves behind: schools, clinics and the people they serve. Survix lets them write surveys on a modest phone, with no connection and no account, keep them on the phone and export them when they need them. It is free, open source, and has no Internet permission, no ads and no tracking.

## Download

**[survix-android.apk](https://github.com/hunixcode/survix/releases/download/v0.1.0/survix-android.apk)**: Android 7.0 or newer, 2.4 MB.

Open the file on the phone and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks.

## What's in 0.1.0

- **Block editor**: sections, free text, single or multiple choice (with an optional "Other"), yes / no, rating on a scale of 2 to 10 points, number with bounds and unit, date. Every question has a label, a help text and a "required" switch, and the editor points out empty labels, missing or duplicated options and reversed bounds.
- **Library**: all the surveys on the phone, newest first, with search, duplicate and delete with undo.
- **Saved as you type**: files are written atomically, so a crash never leaves a half-written survey.
- **Preview**: answer the survey as a respondent would, with required answers and number bounds checked. Nothing typed there is saved.
- **Export, share and import**: `.survix.json` ([documented format](https://github.com/hunixcode/survix/blob/main/docs/format.md)) for one survey or all of them, Markdown ready to print, sharing through any app, and import that never overwrites an existing survey.
- **French and English**, light and dark themes in the Helix design, and a first-launch screen to choose both.

## Good to know

- This APK is signed with a temporary key. When releases move to a permanent key, Survix will have to be uninstalled once before the new version can be installed: export your surveys first (**⋮ → Export all** in the library), then import them again.
- Surveys are kept in the app's private storage and included in the phone's own backup.
